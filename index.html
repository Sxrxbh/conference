📋 Event Breakdown

What Actually Happens at the Event?

The event runs from 9:30 AM to 2:00 PM. Here is exactly what happens, hour by hour.

⚡ Important to Know

The event has TWO things happening at the start (between 10:30 AM and 11:00 AM):

  • Main Track → Where everyone enters, registers, finds their seat
  • Pitch Pit (separate room) → A private session where Micro Founder Pass holders pitch their business idea to the Shark Tank founders for direct feedback

After 11:00 AM, everyone joins together in the main hall. It becomes one single event.

10:30 – 11:00 AM

75–95 attendees arrive, register, and take their seats in the main hall.

The summit officially opens. From this point, all attendees are in the same hall.

Each Shark Tank founder shares their story — what worked, what didn't, what they learned. 60 minutes total.

12:15 – 12:30 PM

15 minutes to meet other founders, chat, and exchange contacts.

12:30 – 1:30 PM

All 5 founders on stage. They answer audience questions and discuss building businesses in India today.

1:45 – 2:00 PM

Wrap-up + Departure

Attendees leave, last conversations happen, lunch for those staying.

🎤 Exclusive Feature

What is the Pitch Pit?

A private session for Serious Founder Passes (₹2,499) — pitch your idea directly to Shark Tank founders.

👥

Up to 15 people enter a separate, smaller room before the main event begins

⏱️

Each person gets 3 minutes to pitch their business idea to the panel of Shark Tank founders

💬

The founders give honest, on-the-spot feedback — what is good, what needs work, what to focus on next

🎥

The session is recorded so the founder can rewatch their pitch and feedback later

🤝

After the Pitch Pit ends (around 11 AM), all 15 Micro Founders join the rest of the audience in the main hall

Why this matters:

Most new founders never get to pitch their idea to anyone who has built a real company. They only pitch to friends and family — who are too nice to tell the truth. The Pitch Pit gives them that one rare chance to get honest feedback from someone who's actually done it.
